### Key Players

- **Mbappé** — 8 goals, joint Golden Boot leader with Messi. Captain, third World Cup, chasing third consecutive final. Ankle concern after Morocco match — cleared fit.
- **Dembélé** — 5 goals, current Ballon d'Or holder. Hat-trick vs Norway. PSG's attacking engine.
- **Michael Olise** — #10 creator, multiple assists throughout tournament.
- **Maignan** — penalty save vs Norway, 3 consecutive knockout clean sheets.

### Tactical Shape

Pragmatic 4-2-3-1 under Deschamps. Double pivot shielding defense. Explosive counter-attacking front line. Roy Keane: "the best team in attack" at this tournament.

Entire PSG attacking quadrangle in form: Dembélé, Barcola, Doué + Mbappé.

### Narrative

Deschamps' farewell tournament after 14 years as coach. France widely seen as co-favourites to lift the trophy.

## Spain — Tournament Run

**Record: W4 D1 L0 | GF 8 GD 1 | Unbeaten**

| Stage | Match | Score | Scorers |
|-------|-------|-------|---------|
| Group | vs Cape Verde | 0-0 | — |
| Group | vs Saudi Arabia | 4-0 | — |
| Group | vs Uruguay | 1-0 | — (Nico Williams injured by De La Cruz tackle) |
| R32 | vs Austria | 3-0 | — |
| R16 | vs Portugal | 1-0 | Merino 90+1' (on at 85', scored 6 min after coming on) |
| QF | vs Belgium | 2-1 | Merino 88' (on at 78', scored within 2 min) |

### Key Players

- **Mikel Oyarzabal** — 4 goals, tournament top scorer for Spain. Also scored Euro 2024 final winner. Clinical.
- **Mikel Merino** — The defining super-sub. Two late match-winners (90+1' vs Portugal, 88' vs Belgium). Both times near-eliminations turned into victories.
- **Lamine Yamal** — 1 goal, most successful dribbles in the tournament. Creative engine on the flank.
- **Álex Baena** — Creative midfield driver.
- **Nico Williams** — Missed Austria and Portugal with adductor injury, returned vs Belgium at 78'. His flank partnership with Yamal was central to Euro 2024.
- **Unai Simón** — 650 minutes without conceding before Belgium's 41' equalizer.

### Tactical Shape

Vertical 4-3-3 under Luis de la Fuente. Rodri as anchor pivot. High defensive line (potentially exploitable by French pace in behind). Transition-focused — sharp departure from tiki-taka era.

First Spain squad since 1950 with no Real Madrid players (Real had disastrous 2025/26 season).

### Narrative

Reigning Euro 2024 champions. Dominant but not spectacular — relying on late goals and defensive solidity. The Merino factor makes them dangerous at any scoreline.

## Match Context

- France have the perfect tournament record (6W, 16 goals) and the individual brilliance (Mbappé 8 goals).
- Spain have the resilience, the tactical discipline, and Merino's clutch factor.
- France dominate in World Cups head-to-head (1-0). Spain edge the recent form (3 of last 4 major meetings).
- High defensive line of Spain vs explosive French counter-attack is the key tactical battle.
- Nico Williams' fitness and Mbappé's ankle are the fitness questions.
- Last meeting: Spain 2-1 France at Euro 2024 semifinal — exact same stage, same rivalry.

This is the rematch.
